Product FAQ: CloudNotes

Q: What is CloudNotes?
A: CloudNotes is a simple note-taking application that syncs across devices
and supports offline editing.

Q: How much does it cost?
A: CloudNotes offers a free tier for up to 100 notes, and a paid plan for
unlimited notes and version history.

Q: Does CloudNotes support markdown?
A: Yes, CloudNotes supports standard markdown formatting including headers,
lists, bold and italic text, and code blocks.

Q: Can I share notes with other users?
A: Yes, any note can be shared via a link with view-only or edit permissions.
