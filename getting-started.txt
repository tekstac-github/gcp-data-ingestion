Getting Started with CloudNotes

Welcome to CloudNotes! This guide walks you through your first five minutes
with the app.

Step 1: Create an account using your email address or single sign-on.
Step 2: Click "New Note" to create your first note.
Step 3: Use the toolbar to format text, add checklists, or insert images.
Step 4: Notes save automatically every few seconds.
Step 5: Install the mobile app to keep your notes in sync on the go.

If you get stuck, visit the help center or contact support from the
Settings menu.
